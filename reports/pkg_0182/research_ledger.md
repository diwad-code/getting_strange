## Research result

No source required a canon rewrite. Research directly changed verification (WASAPI + strict log policy), audio headroom (measured soft limiting), the non-colour threshold readiness cue, evidence labels and terminology review. Human reception, physical controller ergonomics and subjective mix/art quality remain `OPEN-NO-EVIDENCE`/`BLOCKED` rather than fabricated PASS claims.
